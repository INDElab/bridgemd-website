How the work is staffed

The postdoctoral researcher designs, evaluates and implements the methods across the two years, supported by a research software engineer who keeps the software FAIR and integrated into existing data repositories. Each use case has two to three months of dedicated time per year.

See the work packages

BridgeMD — Bridging the distance between data producer and data reuser through automated metadata generation.

A 24-month project (November 2026 – November 2028) led by the Informatics Institute, University of Amsterdam, within the Thematic Digital Competence Centre for Natural and Engineering Sciences (TDCC‑NES).
